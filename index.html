02 / Open source

BurmeseNLP

A dependency-light toolkit for Myanmar-language processing.

I designed a staged, deterministic Python pipeline for normalization, Zawgyi conversion, segmentation, POS tagging, phrase chunking, NER, clause parsing, and corpus export—without mandatory model downloads.

Local AI · Retrieval

Burmese RAG

A Zawgyi-safe, syllable-aware retrieval system for chatting with Burmese documents. It combines multilingual embeddings with syllable n-gram BM25, supports local or hosted LLMs, and returns cited answers.

Research benchmark · LLM efficiency

Myanmar Tokenizer Bench

A reproducible FLORES-200 benchmark showing that popular AI tokenizers use 1.6× to 11.7× more tokens for Burmese than English—affecting cost, speed, and usable context.
