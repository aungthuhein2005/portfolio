About Me

I'm a software engineer at New Generation Myanmar and a Computer Science student at Rangsit University. My work sits at the intersection of product engineering and applied AI: I translate operational requirements into maintainable interfaces, services, data models, and machine-learning workflows. I hold the IP and FE certifications from ITPEC and am completing a Bachelor of Computer Science with an expected graduation in 2029.

In professional delivery, I contribute to education platforms such as training-management and student-information systems, working across user workflows, business rules, and full-stack implementation. My independent engineering work extends from React and Laravel products to desktop security tools and reusable Python packages for Myanmar-language processing.

I am especially focused on systems that demand both software-engineering discipline and model-level reasoning: NLP pipelines, inference interfaces, data preprocessing, and domain-specific tooling. The goal is not simply to demonstrate technologies, but to build components that are understandable, testable, and useful beyond a single demo.
